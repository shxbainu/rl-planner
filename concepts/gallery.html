Herbarium field journal

A botanist's specimen collection. Warm paper, ink-brown type, one rust accent.

The calmest and most readable of the eight. Specimen numbers on the homework chips, wax-seal block letters and a pinned countdown label carry the idea without clutter. Checking off stamps a tilted rust COLLECTED mark.

Fraunces · Newsreader · IBM Plex Mono

Rust is a touch darker than the spec's #B5603C so white text on it passes contrast.

02 / 08

Departure board

A European station at dawn. Split tiles, amber times, condensed sign lettering.

The most fun to look at. The countdown is split-flap tiles that flip when the minute changes, a live clock sits by the homework pill, and each time tile says NOW, NEXT or DEPARTED. Checking off flips the row to a green DEPARTED.

  • #141414 board
  • #F2A93B amber
  • #6FCF7F departed
  • #F2F0EA enamel

Oswald · JetBrains Mono

On phones the clock hides because there's no room beside the pill. Statuses sit under each time instead of in a new column, so rows don't wrap.

Risograph zine

A student-printed two-ink zine. Pink and teal on cream, navy where they overprint.

The loudest one. Classes are told apart by pattern (solid, halftone, outline, hatch) as well as ink, and every class name sits on a paper label so it stays readable. Hand-ruled frames, a pink scribble check and a marker strike-through on check-off.

  • #F7F3EA paper
  • #FF48B0 pink
  • #0078BF teal
  • #3A2C6B navy

Bricolage Grotesque · Space Mono

Pink is too light for small text on cream, so small labels and buttons use navy. Pink stays for big numbers and stickers.

Elegant, and the quietest dark mode. The coordinate grid and stars stay outside the panels, tests get a star sized by how soon they are, and checking off draws a thin gold line to the Done header.

Subtle and easy to live with. A compass rose by the week arrows, red buoys on tests, and the checklist runs down a dotted route where each check fills the waypoint and inks that leg green.

The warmest. Each panel is a packet with a printed band, a tiny flat vegetable and a scalloped foot. Checking off pops a sprout, and the Done counter grows a plant from sprout to bloom as the day goes.

How the switcher works

  • A theme menu (palette icon) sits next to the light/dark button. Your pick is remembered in this browser under its own key; homework, check-offs and countdowns aren't touched.
  • On phones the menu shrinks to just the palette icon beside light/dark, and the date shortens to fit ("Tue, Nov 3").
  • Every text color passed an automated WCAG AA contrast check in all 16 theme and mode combinations. All motion turns off with Reduce Motion.
  • The game window (box score, play-by-play) keeps the original look in every theme for now.
  • Phone proofs show the top of the page. Proofs are scaled down by the browser pane, so the live menu is the best way to judge type.
